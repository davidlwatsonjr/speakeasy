How to practice

  1. Start with a few letters at a time. Compare similar handshapes such as M, N and T.
  2. Practice reading the handshape before revealing the letter. Then reverse the cards and try to form the handshape yourself.
  3. J and Z require movement: trace J with the little finger and Z with the index finger. The illustrations give a motion guide rather than a video.
  4. Spell short names and words, then increase your speed while keeping the handshapes clear.

SpeakEasy tests recognition rather than judging the quality of your signing. The word tests currently omit J and Z. For vocabulary, grammar, facial expression and signing skills, learn with qualified ASL instructors and Deaf signing communities.
